Who We Are

A centre for community-led change

MOCESCOD — the Mowalek Centre for Sustainable Community Development — is a Nigerian non-governmental organisation committed to poverty alleviation, community empowerment and the creation of lasting opportunities for the most vulnerable.

We believe change happens from within communities. Our programmes are designed with, not just for, the people we serve.
