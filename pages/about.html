About MOCESCOD

Rooted in community.
Driven by purpose.

For over a decade, MOCESCOD has worked alongside Nigerian communities to build lasting change through education, health, livelihoods and sustainable development.

Our Story

Why MOCESCOD exists

MOCESCOD, the Mowalek Centre for Sustainable Community Development, was founded on a simple but powerful conviction: that every Nigerian community, no matter how underserved, has the capacity to thrive when given the right support and opportunities.

We work in some of the most vulnerable communities across Nigeria, delivering programmes in youth empowerment, healthcare access, education support, and food security. Our approach is always participatory, we listen before we act.

What sets us apart is our commitment to sustainability. We don't just deliver services, we build local capacity so communities can continue to grow long after our programmes conclude.

Our Foundation

Our Mission

To empower individuals and communities in Nigeria through sustainable development programmes that address poverty, inequality and lack of opportunity at the grassroots level.

Our Vision

A Nigeria where every community has the resources, skills and opportunities to achieve sustainable development and where no person is left behind.

Our Approach

Community-led, data-informed and partnership-driven. We co-design every programme with the people it serves to ensure relevance, ownership and lasting impact.

Core Values

What guides us

01

Integrity

We are transparent in our operations, honest with stakeholders and accountable in all we do.

02

Inclusion

We prioritise the most marginalised: women, youth, persons with disabilities and rural communities.

03

Sustainability

Every programme is designed to outlast our involvement, building local capacity not dependency.

04

Collaboration

We work with government, civil society and communities, because lasting change requires all hands.
